aptihealth — report prototypes

⚠ Prototype only. The real reports will be built in Power BI. These pages exist to agree the metrics.

Every figure is fabricated. What is real is the choice of measure, its definition, the Power BI visual behind it and the gaps we have not closed yet. Each report has a matching document under docs/ naming the business need, the data owner and the business impact for every visual.

Want a visual changed? Say it on the visual. Every report has a Comments button in the bottom right corner — or press Shift+C. Click the visual you want changed, type what should change, and the note stays pinned to that tile with a number; the panel on the right lists every comment on that report. Notes are kept in your own browser, so when you have finished a pass use Copy all or File on GitHub in the panel to send them over.
